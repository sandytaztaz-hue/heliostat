HELIOSTAT is a golden-hour solar-farm tactical FPS where sunlight charges your SUNSHOT — a round-based plant-and-defuse shooter on a white salt flat at golden hour.
## Run it
```sh
node serve.ts
```
Then open http://localhost:8080/game/index.html in desktop Chrome and click to play. HELIOS attacks, UMBRA defends; first to 7 rounds wins. Stand in direct sunlight to charge your CELL — at 100, press F (or middle mouse) to fire a SUNSHOT golden bolt. Facing the low western sun brings glare and a spread penalty, so Site A (ARRAY) is a blind assault into the light while Site B (TOWER) is fought with the sun at your back. Buy weapons between rounds with B (VK-47, Kestrel-9, Lance .50, FRAG-2, SMK-1, armour), plant the charge with E at either site, and watch the minimap, kill feed and radio for your team.
## Controls
WASD move · mouse aim · click shoot · right-click ADS · R reload · E plant (hold) · F SUNSHOT · G frag · Q smoke · B buy menu · Shift sprint · Ctrl crouch.
